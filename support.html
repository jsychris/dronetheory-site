Frequently asked questions

Which tests does it cover?

UK Flyer ID (the CAA's free online test for drones of 100 g and over), UK Flyer ID plus the A2 CofC, and the US FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot knowledge test (UAG). Switch any time from the licence button on Briefing; each licence keeps its own progress.

Is this an official CAA or FAA app?

No. Drone Pilot is an independent study aid and isn't affiliated with the Civil Aviation Authority or the Federal Aviation Administration. Questions and lessons are original, written from the Drone and Model Aircraft Code, 14 CFR Part 107 and public CAA and FAA guidance. Part 107 chart questions use pages from the FAA's public Airman Knowledge Testing Supplement. Always check caa.co.uk or faa.gov/uas.

How closely do the checkrides match the real tests?

Full checkrides use the real format: 40 questions, untimed, pass mark 30 for Flyer ID (the real test is free, online and open book); 60 questions in 2 hours, pass mark 42, weighted across the topics like the real test, for Part 107. Short and custom checkrides and training sorties let you practise in shorter sessions.

How is the readiness gauge worked out?

It combines your accuracy in each topic, weighted by that topic's share of the real test, with the ground school you've completed. Tap the ⓘ next to the gauge for the details.

Does it cover the 2026 UK rules?

Yes. The UK content is written against the rules in force from 1 January 2026: the 100 g Flyer ID threshold, UK class marks, Remote ID and night lighting. Rules change in stages, so always check caa.co.uk before you fly.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No to both. Everything works offline, there's no sign-up, and the app has no adverts or tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I change phone?

Your progress is stored only on your device, so it isn't synced between devices. It is kept in your normal iPhone or iPad backup, so restoring a backup to a new device should bring it with you.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Logbook → Reset progress for this licence and confirm. This clears that licence's XP, streaks, ratings, lessons, checkrides and quick-reference sorting. It can't be undone.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
